Privacy Policy

Poomsae Trainer (iOS) · last updated 20 October 2026

Summary

Poomsae Trainer does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The app does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your training progress (which terms you answered right or wrong, and when you last practiced them) is saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone. It never leaves your device through the app. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this file is part of that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own settings. Deleting the app deletes the file.

Pronunciation

The Korean pronunciation uses Apple's speech synthesis on your device. The app sends no text or audio anywhere.

Apple

The App Store and TestFlight are run by Apple. If you agreed to share analytics or crash reports with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple provides the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. If you send feedback via TestFlight, the developer sees your feedback and the email address of your TestFlight account. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, because a new feature needs data), the new version will be published on this page before the app update is released.
